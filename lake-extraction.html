How these figures were worked out
Source
The board's WaterNSW meter register for logger 120922-3-LID01, - the same data the WaterNSW tab uses, updated with each DAS upload. Daily usage is taken from that register; the hour-by-hour and nightly views come from its hourly readings. Times are local, with daylight saving.
Data gaps
A day missing more than three hours of readings is flagged as a gap in the table. The main outages were 1 October to 3 November 2021 and about a day on 2 to 3 September 2024.
Daily total
Each day's usage is the metered register change for that day, taken straight from the board's WaterNSW data, so this page always matches the WaterNSW tab exactly. Figures are the corrected (divided by ten) volumes.
Nightly run hours
An hour counts as full flow when more than 125 m³ went through the meter in it. Each telemetry reading is the flow since the previous reading, so the 11:00 pm reading is the 10 to 11 pm hour. The daily housekeeping reading part-way through an hour is shared back into that hour. A night is only counted when every hour from 10 pm to 7 am is in the export.
Correction
The telemetry's busiest hours reach about 1,360 kL, which would be 1,360 m³/h, ten times what the pump station can deliver. Divided by 10 they match three CRNE45 pumps at about 135 m³/h. The telemetry is known to be misconfigured and is due to be replaced.
Licence limits
Licence 40SL070163: 1.5 ML/day above 190.25 m AHD, 1.0 ML from 190.05, 0.75 ML from 189.85, 0.5 ML from 189.65, none below. Zone history (level, time of change and lake AHD) from the club's lake dashboard, starting 20 May 2026, with the last recorded change on 2 November 2026; Level 2 is assumed to continue after that. Before 20 May 2026 the licence level is not known, so those days have no shading and are not counted against a limit.
