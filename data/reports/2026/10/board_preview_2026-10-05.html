Projection methodology: Days-to-next-level and days-to-cease use the same day-by-day simulation: monthly BOM open-water evaporation plus daily irrigation demand (irrigation season months only), with no rainfall assumed - a conservative planning figure. October evaporation based on BOM Wagga Wagga Airport long-term average (6.23 mm/day pan × 0.70 lake factor = 4.36 mm/day = 4.90 ML/day at current lake area). Lake surface area adjusts as level changes. The daily table's BOM Avg column is this long-term monthly figure per day, shown beside the station's measured ET (ET is read from the Lake Albert station's solar sensor). All figures are estimates only. Water balance = ET − rainfall; positive = irrigation demand exceeds rainfall (deficit), negative = rainfall exceeds ET (surplus).
